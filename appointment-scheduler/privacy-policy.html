Privacy Policy

Appointment Scheduler (Android app com.fanaandbaqa.appointmentscheduler)
Effective date: 21 November 2026

In short: your clients and bookings stay on your phone unless you sign in to back them up. The free app shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google may collect some device data to show them. We never give your clients, bookings or notes to advertisers, and we do not sell your data.

1. Who we are

Appointment Scheduler is an app for small businesses to keep track of clients, bookings, reminders, holidays and reports. In this policy, "we" means the developer of the app. You can reach us at fanaandbaqa@gmail.com.

2. What you enter in the app

The app stores what you type or choose: client names and phone numbers, bookings, notes, addresses, services and prices, your business profile, holidays, message texts and your settings.

By default all of this is kept only on your phone. If you never sign in, none of it is uploaded.

3. Account and cloud backup (optional)

If you sign in with Google (or Apple on iPhone), we receive your name, email address and profile photo so we can create your account.

After you sign in, your app data is copied to our cloud database (Supabase, hosted in India). This lets you back up, restore and use the same data on more than one phone. The data travels over encrypted connections, and only your account can read it. A daily backup is kept for 7 days and then removed.

4. Ads

The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. Ads appear on some browsing screens (for example reports, analytics and lists) and sometimes after you export a report. There are no ads in the booking form, in reminders or in alarms.

To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app may collect:

  • your phone's advertising ID;
  • your IP address, which gives an approximate location;
  • how you interact with ads (views and taps) and basic app activity;
  • device details and diagnostic data about ad loading.

This data goes to Google, not to us. Google uses it as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

The ad system never receives your clients, phone numbers, bookings, notes or message texts.

Your choices:

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before showing personalised ads, using Google's consent form. You can change your choice any time in Settings → Ad privacy options.
  • On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact menu depends on the phone). Without it, ads are not personalised.
  • Ads are set to a family-safe content rating.

5. Phone permissions

  • Contacts: read only when you tap the contact button, and only the name and numbers of the contact you pick.
  • Notifications, exact alarms and battery settings: used only to show your reminders on time.
  • Photos: only when you choose a screenshot to attach to a bug report.

You can turn any of these off in your phone settings. The app keeps working, but some features may not.

6. Messages, maps and sharing

The app never sends an SMS or WhatsApp message by itself. It opens your SMS or WhatsApp app with the text filled in, and you decide whether to send it. Opening an address passes it to your maps app, and sharing a report or backup passes the file to the app you choose. Those apps follow their own privacy policies.

7. Bug reports (optional)

When you send a bug report, we receive what you write, the type of problem you pick, an optional reply email and an optional screenshot you choose.

If "Include technical details" is on, we also receive the app version, phone model, Android or iOS version, your theme, language and time settings, whether you are signed in, the sync state and the last app errors. Phone numbers and email addresses are removed from those errors. Client names, numbers and bookings are never included, except what is visible in a screenshot you attach.

Bug reports are stored in our cloud database, only the developer can read them, and they are deleted after 180 days.

8. What we don't do

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not use your clients or bookings for ads, profiling or anything other than running the app for you.
  • We do not add analytics or tracking tools of our own. The only third-party data collection is the Google ads SDK described in section 4.

9. Deleting your data and account

  • You can delete single clients and bookings in the app at any time.
  • My account → Delete my data erases everything on your phone. If you are signed in, it also deletes your account, its cloud copy and the bug reports you sent while signed in.
  • Without the app: email fanaandbaqa@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my account" from the email address you signed in with. We delete the account and all its cloud data within 30 days and reply to confirm.
  • Cloud backups are removed with the account. Files you exported or shared elsewhere must be deleted there.

10. Children

The app is made for business owners and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

11. Changes to this policy

When the app's features or data handling change, we update this page and the effective date at the top.

12. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: fanaandbaqa@gmail.com.
